How a case moves through PeopleGuard

  1. 1
    Orchestrator

    Assigns a case ID, classifies, detects POSH and urgent risk at intake, sets the route.

  2. 2
    Policy Expert

    Retrieves handbook sections and quotes them verbatim with section references.

  3. 3
    ER Case Analyst

    Separates allegations from record facts, finds gaps, drafts neutral questions.

  4. 4
    Compliance Reviewer

    Names legal questions to verify — never states deadlines, rates or entitlements.

  5. 5
    Documentation Specialist

    Assembles the 12-part draft report and evidence checklist.

  6. 6
    Quality & Risk Gate

    Checks citations, neutrality, invented law, personal data and escalation; corrects or blocks.

Every report ends with a human review step. Nothing is sent to an employee and no decision is made by the system.
